DEMOCRACY - Opening Script (First Login / New Game)

FADE IN:

EXT. NATIONAL CAPITOL BALCONY - SUNSET (Cinematic Cutscene)
The camera sweeps over a massive crowd waving flags of [Country Name]. Cheers roar. The PLAYER CHARACTER (newly elected Head of State) stands at the podium, finishing their victory speech.

PLAYER CHARACTER (voice, determined, slightly echoing)
"...and together, we will build a stronger, fairer, and more prosperous [Country Name]! Thank you!"

The crowd erupts. Confetti falls. The Player Character waves, then turns and walks back through the grand doors as the sun dips below the horizon.

CUT TO:

INT. PRESIDENTIAL OFFICE - DUSK (Playable Transition)
The heavy oak doors swing open. The Player Character steps into a stately but lived-in office. Tall windows overlook the capital city. A large wooden desk dominates the room, already stacked with briefing folders, a secure phone, and a glowing holographic national dashboard (the main game UI).

Soft orchestral music swells - hopeful yet carrying undertones of responsibility.

An ADVISOR (mid-40s, sharp suit, loyal but pragmatic) stands waiting near the desk.

ADVISOR
"Mr./Ms./President, congratulations. The speech was masterful. The people are energized... for now."

The Advisor gestures to the desk. The camera smoothly transitions to an over-the-shoulder view of the Player Character sitting down. The game UI fades in around the edges of the screen.

ADVISOR (continuing)
"You've inherited the highest office in [Country Name]. This isn't just a title - it's a living simulation of leadership. You'll manage the economy, diplomacy, military readiness, public opinion, infrastructure, and the unpredictable forces of politics and global events. Think of it as real-time strategy meets the delicate art of governance."

UI Pop-up (Tutorial Overlay - Non-intrusive, skippable)
Welcome to Democracy
Hybrid RTS + Political Simulation
Balance growth, stability, and approval. Respond to crises in real time. Negotiate, legislate, and sometimes deceive when necessary - but every choice has consequences.

ADVISOR
"Your first term begins now. Success depends on the challenges you've chosen to face."

The Advisor walks to a large interactive map/table that materializes on the desk (the strategic overview layer).

ADVISOR (tailored to player choices)

On [Difficulty] setting: [For Easy: "We begin from a position of relative strength - solid reserves, moderate support, and few immediate fires."]
[For Medium: "The nation is stable but faces typical pressures - budget constraints, regional tensions, and fluctuating public trust."]
[For Hard: "The situation is precarious. Low approval, strained resources, and multiple simmering crises demand immediate attention."]

Given our [Climate] conditions: [Temperate: "Fertile lands and moderate weather support strong agriculture and steady growth."]
[Arid/Desert: "Water scarcity and extreme heat challenge food security and energy demands."]
[Tropical: "Rich biodiversity but vulnerable to storms, disease vectors, and rapid environmental shifts."]
[Polar/Arctic: "Harsh conditions limit expansion but offer unique mineral and energy opportunities - at a cost."]

ADVISOR (leaning in, serious)
"Your objectives, Mr./Ms./President:

Maintain or grow public approval above critical thresholds.
Drive economic and technological progress while managing debt and inequality.
Ensure national security - through diplomacy, alliances, or military strength.
Navigate random events, opposition parties, international pressures, and scandals.
Ultimately, leave [Country Name] better than you found it... or face the consequences at the ballot box - or worse."

The Advisor straightens up with a small smile.

ADVISOR
"The cabinet, generals, economists, and intelligence chiefs are standing by. The holographic dashboard gives you real-time metrics on GDP, happiness, military readiness, environmental health, and more. Click, drag, and issue directives like in the great strategy games - but here, the 'units' are people, policies, and entire industries."

UI Flash (Goal Summary - Persistent until dismissed)
Primary Win Conditions (4-Year Term):
- Achieve re-election or a strong legacy score
- Reach key national milestones tailored to your vision (Economic Superpower / Green Paradise / Military Fortress / etc.)

Failure States to Avoid:
- Approval drops to zero -> Impeachment / Revolution
- Bankruptcy, invasion, or total collapse

ADVISOR
"Shall we begin with the first briefing, or would you like a quick tour of the core systems first?"

The camera pulls back slightly. The full strategic map lights up across the desk and walls. Soft UI sounds indicate systems coming online. Music transitions into a more active, strategic theme.

FADE TO GAMEPLAY

Additional Implementation Notes (for developers or modders)

Dynamic Text: All bracketed sections should be replaced at runtime based on player selections during character/nation creation.
Voice Acting: The Advisor's lines can be voiced with slight variations per difficulty/climate for replayability.
Pacing: The entire sequence should take 60-90 seconds on first play, with options to skip future playthroughs.
Tutorial Depth: After this, transition into a lightweight guided first turn (e.g., "Address the immediate [Climate]-related issue" or "Review your starting budget").
Tone Nuances: The script balances optimism (new leader energy) with realism (governance is hard). It hints at the social-deduction and intrigue elements through future "scandals" and "negotiation" mentions without spoiling them.
Accessibility: All spoken lines should have subtitles. UI pop-ups remain on-screen until the player interacts.
